## What GSC Audit does

GSC Audit connects to your Search Console property and summarizes:
- period-over-period changes (clicks, impressions, position)
- quick-win keywords (ranking 2 to 5)
- high impressions, low CTR opportunities (title and meta improvements)
- search trends (keywords losing traffic)
- cannibalization (one keyword ranking on multiple pages)
- rank higher opportunities (pages stuck around positions 10 to 20)

It also provides AI suggestions and task-style actions you can execute.

## Quick start

1) Login  
https://ma.copyrocket.ai/login

2) Connect your Search Console property  
- Click connect
- Select the correct property

3) Sync data  
- Click Sync Data to fetch the latest data

4) Choose date range  
- Compare last 7 days, 28 days, or 3 months
- Use longer ranges for stable insight, shorter for recent changes

5) Review the dashboard  
- Clicks change
- Position change
- Impressions change

6) Generate Insights  
- Click Generate Insights to convert data into actions

## Key sections explained

### Period comparison
Shows change vs the previous period.
Use this to detect:
- sudden CTR drops
- ranking movement
- impression growth or decline

### Strikers (positions 2 to 5)
These keywords are close to position 1.
Common actions:
- improve title tag
- improve meta description
- refresh content sections
- add internal links
- answer missing FAQs

### High impressions, low CTR
These pages show on Google often but get fewer clicks than expected.
Fix priority:
1) title tag clarity and benefit
2) meta description promise and specificity
3) align title with intent
4) avoid duplicate titles across pages

### Search trends (losing traffic)
Keywords losing traffic often indicate:
- outdated sections on the page
- competitors updated content
- SERP intent shift

Fix approach:
- update outdated sections
- add missing subtopics
- update examples, dates, stats
- strengthen internal linking

### Cannibalization
One keyword ranking on multiple pages can reduce performance.
Fix options:
- merge pages if intent is the same
- pick one primary page and redirect the weaker one
- re-optimize pages to different intents
- adjust internal links to point to the primary page

### Rank higher opportunities (positions 10 to 20)
Pages that already have impressions and sit in the 10 to 20 range.
These can jump faster than new pages.

Fix approach:
- expand content depth
- improve internal linking from relevant pages
- improve CTR, titles, and meta
- add FAQs and clear headings
- improve topical coverage for that keyword

---

## Best practices

- Start with 3 months for stable patterns
- Fix strikers and low CTR first (fastest ROI)
- Use search trends to decide what to refresh
- Fix cannibalization at the template level if it repeats
- Re-run audit after each fix batch to confirm improvement

## Troubleshooting

### Property is not showing
- Confirm you are logged into the right Google account
- Ensure you have access permissions in Search Console

### Data looks empty or too small
- Try switching the date range
- Confirm site is verified and receiving impressions

## FAQ

### How often should I run this?
- Weekly for active sites
- Monthly for stable sites
- After major content updates
